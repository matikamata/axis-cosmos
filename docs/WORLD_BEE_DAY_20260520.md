# World Bee Day 2026 Dedication

## The Flower Opens

On 20 May 2026, AXIS-Cosmos was made public as a local-first graph and constellation lab for AXIS preservation archaeology.

World Bee Day reminds us of pollinators: small beings whose quiet work connects fields, forests, flowers, and future harvests.

AXIS-Cosmos is offered in that same spirit:  
not as Canon,  
not as authority,  
but as a small open flower for future helpers, reviewers, builders, and caretakers.

May the Bees find the Flower.  
May useful connections become visible.  
May preservation work continue with care.
